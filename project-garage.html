Garage Beer

Proyecto master UX/UI Design, ELISAVA

Garage Beer is a craft brewery located in Barcelona. The goal was to digitally transform the experience delivered by the bar in a way that responds to the needs of expert beer customers as well as beginners.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Scope

UX design · UI design · Graphic design

Year

2021 – 2022

Client

Garage Beer, Barcelona

01. User Experience

Methodology

The central axis for the development of this project was the user. We believe that through empathy and understanding of their needs we will be able to develop a close discourse with a unique and attentive vision of their consumers.

User Centered Design

This is why we chose Human Centered Design as the framework. It's investigative method based on the active participation of users is adapted to the team vision and goals to be achieved.

Research techniques

The different techniques used in the research have been the Benchmark, Desk Research and Interviews.

The Desk Research was essential to develop a terminological and conceptual context about the current situation of beer and its market. It provided us with important data that was later used in decision-making.

The interviews have helped us to understand the reality of the user, along with their thoughts, feelings, needs and frustrations.

The benchmark has helped us to get inspiration regarding the architecture of the information and the interactions that can be useful to the user when navigating the page.

Architecture

The architecture was defined according to the results of the following stages:

  • Content Mapping
  • User Journey
  • Sitemap
  • Wireframe

Then with this information plus the previous research of people, an architectural proposal for the site was arrived to these result.
