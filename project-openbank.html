Openbank — New App

UX/UI Design

After 10 years comes this major update of the App with a complete new design, making it more intuitive, accessible and secure.

Role

UX/UI Designer

Deliverables

App redesign · New features

Team

Product Designer · Product Owner

Client

Santander / Openbank

This major update follows a phased release strategy, enabling updates to be rolled out section by section. This method ensures a gradual and controlled implementation, making it easier to evaluate results and integrate enhancements based on user feedback.

MVP N°1 — Login

Safer, more intuitive, and accessible for everyone.

In this initial phase, significant updates have been made to the Login, enhancing its security, usability, and accessibility. Additionally, the global positioning of the application has been revamped, optimizing its design and structure to provide users with a smoother and more consistent experience from the very first interaction.

New features have also been introduced, such as the ability to customize product positions and edit Quick Actions to better suit each user's individual needs.
